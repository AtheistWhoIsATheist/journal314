# Residue Management Protocol (RMP)

**Purpose**: Ensure every unresolved paradox, failure, or ambiguity from one φ-Loop becomes the seed for the next loop.  
**Artifacts**: `residue_ledger.json` (append-only), Residue IDs (RZ-YYYYMMDD-####).

**Lifecycle**
1) Capture: record residue with full context (source, pass, protocol, excerpts, metrics).
2) Prioritize: rank by impact (architectonic, ethical, phenomenological, logical).
3) Assign: map to next φ-Loop entry strategy (e.g., P-1 inversion, P-4 stress test).
4) Close or Recurse: mark resolved if structural gain achieved; else deepen via sub-loop.

**Update Rules**
- Immutable history; edits append a new version with linkage to prior.
- Crosslink to Journal314/REN references where applicable.
- Auto-promote recurring residues to 'Open Problems' list.
