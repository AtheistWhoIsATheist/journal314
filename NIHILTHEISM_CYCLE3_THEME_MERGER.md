### REVISED THEME DEFINITION

**4. APOPHATIC/UNKNOWABLE** 

- **Definition**: Knowledge of the divine/ultimate through systematic negation, rational self-critique, or acknowledgment of fundamental unknowability; what cannot be said, known, or rationally grasped
- **Inclusion Criteria**: 
- Systematic via negativa methodology (classical apophatic)
- Recognition that reason/language are inadequate to ultimate truth
- Unknowability positioned as positive path rather than mere limitation
- Silence, paradox, or conceptual breakdown as revelatory
- **Overlap Policy**: true (thinkers may use both rational and mystical approaches)
